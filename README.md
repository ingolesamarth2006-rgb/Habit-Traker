# ✨ What is Growth Map?

**Growth Map** is a personal growth and productivity system designed to manage different parts of life from one interface.

Instead of using separate applications for:

- Habit tracking
- Study planning
- Coding practice
- Project management
- Journaling
- Health tracking
- Resources
- Daily planning

Growth Map attempts to connect them into one unified ecosystem.

The goal is simple:

> **Open one system and know what matters today, what you are working toward, and how much progress you are actually making.**

---

# 🧠 Core Philosophy

Most productivity applications answer questions like:

> "Did you complete this task?"

Growth Map tries to answer deeper questions:

> "What are you trying to become?"

> "What did you actually learn?"

> "Which project is moving forward?"

> "Where are you spending your time?"

> "Are today's actions connected to your long-term goals?"
